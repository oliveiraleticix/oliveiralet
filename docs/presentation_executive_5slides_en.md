# Executive Presentation (EN) - 5 slides

## Slide 1 - Context and objective
**Title:** Calypso x SAP reconciliation automation (Futures)  
**Core message:** We turned a manual routine into a daily, reliable, action-oriented flow.

**Suggested script (20-30s):**  
"Our goal was straightforward: reduce operational friction and speed up pre-closing readiness through daily automation."

---

## Slide 2 - Pain and opportunity
**Title:** What we needed to fix
- Manual and repetitive process
- Higher operational risk from manual checks
- Slower mismatch detection cycle

**Impact:** ~**1.5 hours saved per week**

---

## Slide 3 - Solution delivered
**Title:** What is now in production
- Daily Databricks Job
- One parameterized notebook per entity (**BR11, BR12, BR28**)
- Automatic Slack status with account-level details
- Automatic owner mentions when differences are found

---

## Slide 4 - Business outcomes
**Title:** Value already captured
- Faster Futures pre-closing
- Higher operational predictability
- Daily team transparency
- Scalable structure for new entities/rules

---

## Slide 5 - Next step (call to action)
**Title:** How we sustain the gains
- Use Slack channel as the official daily source
- Treat warnings on the same day
- Evolve to historical dashboard and severity SLA

**Closing line:**  
"Automation removed low-value effort and gave time back for higher-impact decisions."
